A LITTLE OFFLINE PUZZLE

Circuit Break

⚡

Rotate the wires. Bring power from S to T without an open end along the powered route.

How to play

Click or tap a tile to rotate it clockwise. With a keyboard, Tab to a tile and press Enter or Space. Green wires carry power. S and T rotate too. Reset restores the starting arrangement.

Three handcrafted circuits · No timer · No account needed to play. No automatic network requests or saved personal data.
Progress lasts until this page is closed or reloaded. Prototype v0.1.2.
