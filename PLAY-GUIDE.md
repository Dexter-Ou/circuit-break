# Circuit Break: download and play

Rotate the wires to bring power from **S** to **T**, with no open ends along the powered route. Three handcrafted circuits; no timer.

1. [Download the fixed v0.1.2 source ZIP](https://codeload.github.com/Dexter-Ou/circuit-break/zip/870fbdc5b7da1c71f77819355b27503a8927c77f).
2. Extract the ZIP. Open the extracted folder and open `index.html` in a current browser. Open the extracted file, rather than a preview inside the archive.
3. Click or tap a tile to rotate it clockwise. With a keyboard, Tab to a tile and press Enter or Space. S and T rotate too.
4. Follow the green powered wires from S. Turn tiles until the route reaches T with no open ends. Then choose **Next circuit**.

**Reset circuit** restores the current circuit's starting arrangement and zero turns. Reloading starts from circuit one; progress is not saved.

No installation, server or account is needed to play. The game has no automatic network requests, telemetry or external assets. The download requires an internet connection; the extracted game works offline. Automated Chromium keyboard checks at 320 × 640 covered all three revised circuits, reset and reload. Physical touch devices, screen readers and other browsers have not been tested for this revision.

[Report a bug or share play feedback](https://github.com/Dexter-Ou/circuit-break/issues): include your browser/device, the circuit and what happened. Posting requires internet and a GitHub account. Reports are public, so omit personal details. Support is best effort, with no promised response time.
